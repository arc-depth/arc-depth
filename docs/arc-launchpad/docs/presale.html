FAQ

Questions

Where does my money go?

Into the presale contract on Arc. It has no owner and no withdraw function: the only ways out are the pool (when the round fills) and refunds (when it does not). When the pool opens, all $15,000 becomes buy-side liquidity and can never be removed, by anyone, us included.

What if the round does not fill?

When the 30 days end, every buyer of that round gets their USDC back. The bot sends refunds in batches; you can also take yours yourself with one click on this page. The next round starts immediately, with a fresh $15,000 target.

Why is the presale price below the opening?

Presale buyers pay $0.00006 and the pool opens at $0.00007. They take the risk of waiting and their money is what makes the pool deep, so they come in cheaper.

Can the founder sell early?

No ARCP exists in any wallet until the pool opens. The founder's 15% sits in a contract that pays 75M at the opening and the rest monthly over 12 months, nothing faster.

What does ARCP earn?

Stakers receive USDC from trading fees and a weekly ARCP release from the staking pool. A share of every trade on ARC PULSE buys ARCP and burns it, so the supply only goes down.

What happens to the earlier ARCP?

This presale launches a new ARCP contract. The address shown on this page is the only presale and the only new token; the previous contract stays where it is.

Arc mainnet, chain 5042. No contract here is upgradeable; none has a withdraw, pause or mint function.
