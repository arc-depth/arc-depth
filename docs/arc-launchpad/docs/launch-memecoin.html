How to launch a memecoin on 13 chains with ARC PULSE

Launch once from the chain you hold, and your token trades from all thirteen at one price, in one pool on Arc, with its liquidity locked and a collateral basket behind it. Here is every step, and every fee, before you start.

2 · Step by step

An ARC PULSE launch in three steps

1

Name it, set the first buy

Name, ticker and an image. Then a first buy from $20 to $500: 2% is the launch fee and the rest becomes your tokens at the opening price ($3,500 for the whole supply of 1,000,000,000), before anyone else.

2

Back it with a collateral basket

Pick 1 to 5 tokens to back yours, and a collateral tax of 1% to 5%: that share of every trade goes into the basket. Holders can redeem their share of the basket at any time instead of selling.

3

Review and launch

See the fees, pick the chain you pay from, and sign once in your wallet. Every fee is shown before you sign.

Tips for a good launch

  • A name and ticker people remember. Short, easy to say, easy to search.
  • An image that reads at small sizes. It shows as a round avatar in lists and on the chart.
  • A basket that fits your story. The basket is what holders can redeem against; choose tokens you would want behind yours.
  • Share the token page, not a screenshot. It carries the chart, the contract address and the buy box from every chain.

3 · What the contracts enforce

Rules nobody can change after launch

  • One pool, one priceEvery ARC PULSE token trades in one Uniswap v4 pool on Arc. Buy or sell from any of thirteen chains and you meet the same price.
  • Liquidity locked from block oneThe pool's liquidity is locked in the pool contract. The trade contracts have no owner, pause or proxy.
  • Fixed supply1,000,000,000 tokens. No mint, no owner.
  • A collateral vault per tokenEach basket line waits as USDC on Arc until it reaches $50; then its vault buys that asset on its own chain, for this token only.
  • Anti-snipeA buy in the launch's first second pays 99%, then a quarter of that each second, nothing from the fifth. It goes back into the pool.
  • Whale shieldSales in one block above 0.5% of the circulating supply pay the excess (at most 3% more), into the basket.

4 · Every fee

Where every trade goes

2% of your first buy at launch. On every trade after it: 2.2%, split as below, plus the 1–5% collateral tax you chose.

5 · Chains

Launch from the chain you hold

Pay for your launch from Arc, Base, Solana, BNB Chain, Robinhood Chain, Arbitrum, Ethereum, Avalanche, Monad, HyperEVM, Sui, Aptos or Elysium, in one signature, with that chain's own gas token. The token lives on your chain as its twin (from Elysium, it is delivered to the same address on Arc), and you pick which other chains it exists on from the start; each twin costs its real LayerZero fee, shown before you pay, and a chain left out can get its twin later from anyone.

Want the token itself to live on Sui or Aptos? ARC PULSE launches those too, with their own pool and a vault for the collateral, paid in native USDC from that wallet.

7 · After the launch

Share it, and collect your income

  • Share the token page. It opens the chart and the buy box for every chain; your launch also appears in All launches and in Discover.
  • Collect your 0.30%. Your slice of every trade collects on Arc in USDC. Launched from another chain? Send it on with one click from Trade (buy, sell, redeem → founder's trading income).
  • Invite people. Referrals give you a link to share.

FAQ

Launching, answered

How much does it cost to launch a memecoin on ARC PULSE?

You choose a first buy between $20 and $500. 2% of it is the launch fee; the rest buys your tokens at the opening price, before anyone else. Each extra twin chain you pick adds its own LayerZero fee, shown before you pay.

Which chains can I launch from?

Any of thirteen: Arc, Base, Solana, BNB Chain, Robinhood Chain, Arbitrum, Ethereum, Avalanche, Monad, HyperEVM, Sui, Aptos and Elysium. Every ARC PULSE token trades in one pool on Arc, so every chain sees the same price.

What does the creator earn?

0.30% of every trade of your token, paid in USDC. It collects on Arc, and if you launched from another chain you can send it on to that chain with one click.

Is the liquidity locked?

Yes. The pool's liquidity is locked in the pool contract from block one, and the trade contracts have no owner, pause or proxy, so the fees and the lock cannot be changed.

Can I launch on pumpfun or another launchpad instead?

Yes, with Warp: pick a launchpad ranked by live volume and launch there from your own wallet. Montra on Elysium is live; pumpfun, Raydium LaunchLab, Meteora, Flap and Pons are connected and coming. Warp's fee is 0.5% of your first buy.
