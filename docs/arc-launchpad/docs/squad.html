The Pulse Squad: one mascot for every chain

ARC PULSE runs on thirteen chains, and each one has a mascot. Halo is live on Elysium; the others launch one by one through Warp. Pulsy, the heartbeat of the squad, looks after Arc and guides Ethereum.
