Hot tokens · 13 chains

Hot tokens on 13 chains, safety-checked

The tokens moving right now on 13 chains, after a safety check on each one. Buy any of them with USDC or the gas token you already hold, from your own wallet.

Open in app Market

In short

What Hot tokens is

Hot tokens is a list of trending and new tokens across 13 chains. Every hour it takes trending and new pools from GeckoTerminal, keeps those above a floor for liquidity, volume and age, and runs a safety check: RugCheck on Solana, GoPlus and honeypot.is on EVM chains. Tokens that fail are not shown; lesser findings appear as a note on the card.

On the screen

What each row tells you

Safety badge

checked means the token passed; note means it passed with a finding shown on the card, such as a buy or sell tax; unchecked means no check exists for that chain yet.

Market numbers

Chain, market cap, liquidity, 24-hour volume and age, with prices refreshed live in your browser.

Tabs

Trending, New, Gainers, your Watchlist, and Hotweet: tokens named in posts from accounts we watch.

Buy box

Pay with USDC or the gas token of any supported chain. The cost is quoted from the pool's own price before you sign.

Token page

Tap a token for its chart, Pattern Hunter on its own history, and the Indicator scorecard.

Watchlist

Star a token to follow it. The watchlist lives in your browser only; nothing about you is sent to ARC PULSE.

Step by step

How to use it

1

Pick a chain or search

Filter by any of 13 chains, or search by name, symbol or contract address.

2

Read the card

Check the safety badge, the liquidity and the notes before anything else.

3

Buy from your wallet

Choose what you pay with, see the quote and every fee, then sign. Sui and Aptos tokens open their chart and trade in a Sui or Aptos wallet.

What a safety check is not

  • Not a guarantee. A check looks for known risks such as honeypots, blocked selling or high taxes. A token that passes can still lose most of its value.
  • Memecoins are volatile. Prices on this list can move 50% or more in a day. Read the risk disclosure.
  • Not a recommendation. Being on the list means a token is moving and passed the check, nothing more.

FAQ

Hot tokens, answered

How often is the list updated?

The list is rebuilt every hour; prices on the page are live.

Which safety checks run?

RugCheck for Solana tokens, and GoPlus plus honeypot.is for tokens on EVM chains. Tokens that fail are left off the list.

What can I pay with?

USDC or the gas token of any supported chain, from your own wallet. The cost is shown before you sign.

Does ARC PULSE store my watchlist?

No. It is kept in your browser only. ARC PULSE keeps no accounts, cookies or analytics.

Is a checked token safe to buy?

Not necessarily. The check removes known traps; it says nothing about where the price goes next.
