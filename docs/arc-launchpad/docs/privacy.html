Privacy

No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no trackers, and no server of ours. This site is static files. We keep nothing about you and cannot see who visits or who connects a wallet.

On this page

  1. What we collect
  2. What your browser keeps (on your device only)
  3. Who your browser talks to
  4. Public by nature
  5. Telegram and X
  6. Questions

1. What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no email list, no analytics script and no tracking pixel. We do not store which wallet connects, what you trade or which pages you open.

2. What your browser keeps (on your device only)

  • Display preferences (view, filters, theme) and markers that let a page finish a pending cross-chain step after a reload.
  • Whether you have ticked the perps risk confirmation.
  • If you use WalletConnect: its session keys. "Disconnect and forget this device" wipes them.
  • If you enable trading on the Hyperliquid page: the trading key for your account (it can trade, not withdraw). Remove it from the trade page or on Hyperliquid.
  • The Time Machine runs in your browser: your entry, stop and target are never sent anywhere.

This data never leaves your browser. Clearing the site's data removes it.

3. Who your browser talks to

  • Blockchain RPC providers (Arc, Base and the other chains; publicnode, drpc and the chains' own): to read balances and prices and to send transactions you sign. They see your IP address and the addresses involved.
  • Hyperliquid (api.hyperliquid.xyz): market data, and when you trade, your address, orders and account. Hyperliquid's records are public by design.
  • relay.link and Circle: only for cross-chain quotes, payments and attestations.
  • DexScreener and GeckoTerminal: token prices and charts. WalletConnect (Reown): only if you choose it.
  • GitHub Pages serves these files and keeps standard server logs, which we cannot access.

The site's security policy blocks connections to anything not on its list.

4. Public by nature

Blockchain transactions are public. Any address that trades, launches or redeems is visible on-chain to everyone, and Hyperliquid shows every account's positions publicly. We add nothing to that.

Our hourly data jobs read public market data and the public positions of large Hyperliquid accounts, and publish them only as totals per market and price level: no address or single position is published.

5. Telegram and X

Our Telegram helper bot (@arcpulse_squad_bot) keeps the text of messages sent to it or posted in the group for up to 7 days so the team can answer, then deletes them automatically. It does not store names, usernames or user IDs. Messages with links or contract addresses from non-admins are deleted as a scam guard. X (Twitter) and Telegram have their own privacy policies.

6. Questions

Ask on X or Telegram. As we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to export or delete; to remove what your browser keeps, clear this site's data in your browser.
