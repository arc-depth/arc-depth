1. What ARC PULSE is

ARC PULSE ("we", the ARC PULSE team) publishes a static website and open-source smart contracts. The site has no accounts and no server that holds your data or your funds.

We never hold your assets or your keys. Every transaction is built in your browser and signed by your wallet. Transactions on a blockchain are final: we cannot reverse, cancel or refund them.

  • You must be at least 18 years old and able to enter a binding agreement.

  • Perpetual futures (the Trade Hyperliquid perps page) are not offered to US persons or to anyone where such products are restricted for them. You confirm this before your first order.
  • You are responsible for following the laws that apply to you, including taxes.

3. Third-party protocols

Many features route to protocols we do not run and cannot control: Hyperliquid (perpetual futures), relay.link (cross-chain payments), Circle CCTP, LayerZero, Montra and other launchpads (Warp), decentralised exchanges, wallets and RPC providers. Their own terms and risks apply. If one of them fails, pauses, changes its rules or loses funds, we cannot make it good.

4. Fees

Fees are shown before you sign. They include, depending on the feature: the launch fee and trading fees of the ARC PULSE pool, the 0.5% Market fee, Warp's fee where one applies, and an ARC PULSE fee of 0.05% on orders placed on Hyperliquid through this site (a Hyperliquid builder fee you approve once and can revoke at any time). Third parties charge their own fees and network fees. The Hyperliquid fee income is split 40% ARCP burn / 30% development / 20% marketing / 10% founder.

Models describe possible futures, never the future. You make every decision and carry its result.
