FAQ

Halo, answered

What is Halo ($HALO)?

Halo is the first of the Pulse Squad, ARC PULSE's twelve mascots, one for each chain. Halo stands for Elysium and its memecoin, $HALO, launched on 12 Nov 2026 through ARC PULSE's Warp on Montra.

What is the $HALO contract address?

The $HALO contract on Elysium is 0xebEe0B053569333d72006d5dA46362eeaD1Ccd3B. Always check this address before you buy; the same address is shown on Montra and on ARC PULSE.

How do I buy $HALO?

Open $HALO on Montra (montramarkets.xyz) with an Elysium wallet, or open it in ARC PULSE's Trade page to see the chart and the routes from the chain you hold. Montra charges 2% per trade.

Does the ARC PULSE team hold $HALO?

Yes, and it is labelled: the team wallet 0x89189C84DC95d0520AEeC9c8cF3BA5B9d42611D0 holds 31,436,474 HALO, about 3.1% of the 1,000,000,000 supply, from the launch's first buy.
