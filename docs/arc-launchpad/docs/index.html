The platform

Understand the system.

01Create the token

Pick a chain, a name, the dollar collateral and a 1-5% floor tax. The founder's buy lands at the opening price — no discount, nothing to front-run.

02The vault is born

All of the collateral goes into this token's own vault. Each token represents a proportional claim on its collateral basket. Market and collateral values can change.

03The pool opens, locked

A Uniswap v4 pool opens with real dollars on the bid side. Its hook rejects every liquidity removal, so the pool can't be pulled.

04Trade, feed, redeem

Every buy and sell sends the floor tax into the vault. Holders can burn and take their share of the vault whenever they want.

Collateral you choose, on the chain you launch from

Paste the contract address of any token on the chain you launch from (Arc, Robinhood, Base, Arbitrum, Avalanche or Monad). Your collateral deposit, 0 or more, is converted into that token in the launch transaction, and the vault holds it. Every trade's tax then flows in as dollars. Redeeming burns tokens for a share of both, so the floor is real tokens in the vault, never a promise. Leave the field empty to keep the collateral in dollars.

Collateral vaults

Each token has its own vault. There is exactly one way out: burning tokens. The burn shrinks supply and payout shrinks collateral in the same proportion, so the remaining holders retain their proportional claim. Collateral values, execution costs and market prices can change.

FAQ

A few things worth knowing.

Can anyone withdraw the money?

No. The vault has no withdraw function, and it carries no instruction that could change its code, so one cannot be added later.

Can the token go to zero?

Yes. Market price and collateral value can fall. Collateral backing is a redeemable claim, not a guaranteed price or a guarantee against losses.

Can the founder sell everything?

Yes — their tokens are theirs. What they can't do is pull the pool's money or the vault's collateral, and their sale's tax goes into the vault too.

Has it been audited?

Not externally. 811 automated checks run against real mainnet state, and every defect they found is documented in the code. That is not a substitute for an audit.

Can I skip the fee through an aggregator?

No. The hook is part of the pool's identity, so it runs whichever interface you come through — including exact-output trades.

Are stock-backed launches available?

No. Tokenized stocks on Robinhood Chain may not be offered to persons in the United States, and are restricted in Canada, the UK and Switzerland. A launch backed by them would need identity checks and transfer limits that a permissionless pool cannot enforce, so ARC PULSE does not offer one. The current launch form supports collateral baskets; asset availability and restrictions are shown in the app.

Is this investment advice?

No. These are volatile tokens. Collateral and locked liquidity do not guarantee returns or prevent losses.
