How it works

One transaction. Four things at once.

01Create the token

Pick a chain, a name, the collateral and a 1-5% floor tax. The founder's buy lands at the opening price — no discount, nothing to front-run.

02The vault is born

All of the collateral goes into this token's own vault. Floor = vault ÷ circulating supply, and it never drops when someone redeems.

03The pool opens, locked

A Uniswap v4 pool opens with real dollars on the bid side. Its hook rejects every liquidity removal, so the pool can't be pulled.

04Trade, feed, redeem

Every buy and sell sends the floor tax into the vault. Holders can burn and take their share of the vault whenever they want.

Collateral vaults

Each token has its own vault. There is exactly one way out: burning tokens. The burn shrinks supply and payout shrinks collateral in the same proportion, so the floor never moves for whoever stays. If the market price drops below the floor, buying there and redeeming is profit — so the market defends it.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Can anyone withdraw the money?

No. The vault has no withdraw function, and it carries no instruction that could change its code, so one cannot be added later.

Can the token go to zero?

Only if its collateral does. A USDC- or USDG-backed floor is in dollars; one backed by BTC moves with BTC. What is guaranteed is that the ratio doesn't break.

Can the founder sell everything?

Yes — their tokens are theirs. What they can't do is pull the pool's money or the vault's collateral, and their sale's tax goes into the vault too.

Has it been audited?

Not externally. 811 automated checks run against real mainnet state, and every defect they found is documented in the code. That is not a substitute for an audit.

Can I skip the fee through an aggregator?

No. The hook is part of the pool's identity, so it runs whichever interface you come through — including exact-output trades.

Is this investment advice?

No. These are memecoins. A floor doesn't make one a good investment; it makes the location of zero something you can know.
