Pattern Hunter · Perps

Pattern Hunter: 12 chart patterns, 1 hour to monthly

A scan for classic chart patterns on the 50 most traded Hyperliquid markets, every 2 hours. Each card draws the pattern, its entry, target and stop, and how that pattern did in the past on that timeframe.

Open in app Trade perps

In short

What Pattern Hunter is

Pattern Hunter is a scanner. Every 2 hours it reads the price history of the 50 most traded Hyperliquid perpetual markets on 1-hour, 4-hour, daily, weekly and monthly charts, looks for 12 classic chart patterns, and lists fresh breakouts and patterns still forming. It describes what it finds; it does not predict prices.

On the screen

The 12 patterns it looks for

Double top & double bottom

Two peaks or two troughs at about the same level, with a breakout through the line between them.

Head and shoulders & inverse

Three peaks (or troughs) with the middle one most extreme, broken through the neckline.

Triangles

Ascending, descending and symmetrical: prices squeezed between two converging lines.

Wedges

Rising and falling wedges: both lines slope the same way and converge.

Channels

Prices moving between two parallel lines, until a close outside them.

Bull & bear flags

A sharp move followed by a short pause against it.

Step by step

How a card is built

1

Find the pattern

Pivots are only counted once they are confirmed, so a pattern never uses prices that had not happened yet.

2

Mark the levels

A breakout is the first close through the pattern's line. The target is the classic measured move; the stop is where the pattern is proven wrong.

3

Show the record

Each card shows how that pattern did on that timeframe in a backtest on 21 coins since 2017, including where the average was about zero.

Read it the right way

  • A record, not a forecast. Past results of a pattern say nothing certain about the next breakout. The page shows weak records as plainly as strong ones.
  • Short timeframes are noisy. On 1-hour and 4-hour charts the backtest average was about zero before fees, and the cards say so.
  • Patterns already proven wrong are removed from the list.
  • Nothing is sent until you sign. Opening a card's trade link only fills the Perps order form for you to review.

FAQ

Pattern Hunter, answered

Which markets does it scan?

The 50 most traded perpetual markets on Hyperliquid, every 2 hours, on 1-hour, 4-hour, daily, weekly and monthly charts.

Where does the backtest come from?

A test of the same pattern rules on 21 coins, using Binance price history since 2017 and Hyperliquid's own, split into an older 70% and a recent 30%.

Does it work on tokens outside Hyperliquid?

Yes, in part: every Hot tokens page runs Pattern Hunter on that token's own hourly history.

Is a breakout a signal to trade?

No. It is a level to watch with a recorded history. Nothing here is advice.
