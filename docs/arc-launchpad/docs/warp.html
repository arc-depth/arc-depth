How it works

Three steps

1

See where the volume is

Warp's radar ranks every launchpad on thirteen chains by what traded there in the last 24 hours.

2

Pick a launchpad

Take the busiest on your chain, or any other. Name, ticker, image and your opening buy are set once.

3

Sign in your own wallet

The launch goes straight to that launchpad's contracts. Your token lives there, with that launchpad's own pool and rules.

Fees

0.5% of your first buy

0.3% to ARC PULSE and 0.2% set aside for ARCP, which buys ARCP and burns it once its pool is open. That is all Warp takes.

The launchpad's own fees stay exactly as that launchpad sets them, and are shown before you sign. On Montra you keep 90% of the creator side.

Looking for a pumpfun alternative?

Two ways to launch

Warp puts you on the launchpad of your choice, ranked by live volume, from one form.

ARC PULSE's own launchpad is a different model: one pool on Arc that every one of thirteen chains trades, locked liquidity, a collateral basket behind every token, and 0.30% of every trade to the creator.

FAQ

Warp, answered

What is Warp?

Warp is ARC PULSE's launcher for other launchpads. It ranks the memecoin launchpads on ARC PULSE's thirteen chains by live volume and launches your token on the one you pick, on that launchpad's own contracts, signed in your own wallet.

What does Warp cost?

0.5% of your first buy: 0.3% to ARC PULSE and 0.2% set aside for ARCP. The launchpad's own fees are its own and are shown before you sign.

Which launchpads can I launch on?

Montra on Elysium is live. pumpfun, Raydium LaunchLab and Meteora on Solana, Flap on BNB Chain, Robinhood and Monad, and Pons on Robinhood are connected and open with their first launch through Warp.

Is Warp a pumpfun alternative?

Warp lets you choose: launch on the launchpad that suits your chain. And ARC PULSE's own launchpad is a different model: one pool on Arc, buyable from thirteen chains, with a collateral basket behind every token.
