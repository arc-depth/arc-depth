# Hype and Gaps: Memecoin / Launchpad Landscape (as of 16 Oct 2026)

Research for ARC PULSE. Read-only desk research (web search + fetch). Numbers are as reported by the cited sources; several are vendor/press claims and were not independently verified.

## 1. What is getting attention (2025-2026)

### Solana: pump.fun vs bonk.fun
- **pump.fun** lost its monopoly: share fell from ~90% to as low as 24%; LetsBonk passed 50% (51% vs 39.9%) and at one point did 16,797 daily launches vs pump's 7,537. [Yahoo/bonk.fun 55%](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/bonk-fun-grabs-55-solana-072753112.html), [Cryptopolitan](https://www.cryptopolitan.com/letsbonk-on-track-for-meme-market/), [FXEmpire](https://www.fxempire.com/forecasts/article/bonk-price-news-bonk-could-rise-by-50-as-letsbonk-overthrows-pump-fun-1536172)
- pump.fun fees fell to $31.8M in Jan 2026 (-75.6% YoY). [Cryptobriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/pumpfun-holder-rewards-cashback-deprecated/)
- **What brought pump.fun back:**
  - **Livestreams + creator fees.** Dynamic creator fee up to 0.95%, falling as market cap grows. Creator fees rose about 10x. Daily fees hit >$2M in Sep 2026, versus a normal $100-150k. PUMP passed a $3B market cap and the team said it was "nibbling the lunch" of Rumble and Kick. [DL News](https://www.dlnews.com/articles/markets/pump-fun-token-3bn-market-cofounder-nibble-lunch-rumble-kick/), [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/pumpfun-memecoin-streams-explode-as-creators-pocket-record-earnings-in-last-week/), [PANews](https://www.panewslab.com/en/articles/1ccedaf9-ffd4-4125-ae3f-b03fb3a579f8)
  - **Cashback Coins (Feb 2026).** At launch the creator picks, irreversibly, between creator fees and 0.3% cashback to traders. On 12 Sep 2026 these were replaced by **Holder Rewards**, which pay fees pro-rata to holders several times per hour. [TradingView/Cointelegraph](https://www.tradingview.com/news/cointelegraph:acc947ce3094b:0-pump-fun-rolls-out-trader-cashbacks-in-tweak-to-memecoin-model/), [Cryptobriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/pumpfun-holder-rewards-cashback-deprecated/)
  - **BOOST mode.** For 5 minutes after migration, about 17.6 SOL of migration capital buys the token back and burns it. pump.fun puts "dead liquidity" at about $100M a year. [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/pump-fun-boost-mode-explained)
  - **Fee splitting.** Creator fees can be split across up to 10 wallets (Bags allows up to 100). More than 98% of revenue goes to PUMP buybacks (about $213M retired). [bex.co](https://bex.co/blog/2026/04/22/meme-launchpad-2-pump-fun-letsbonk-anti-sniper-bonding-curve-professionalization), [Coin Bureau](https://coinbureau.com/analysis/best-memecoin-launchpads)
  - **Fair Launch Shield.** CAPTCHA plus anti-bot settings. LetsBonk uses Bonk Points to give verified wallets better access. [bex.co](https://bex.co/blog/2026/04/20/meme-launchpad-2-pump-fun-letsbonk-anti-sniper-reputation)

### Believe / Launchcoin
- Believe had a viral run in 2025 (+27,000%). From May to Oct 2025 the token then fell about 99%. The promised "flywheel" buybacks never happened, and there is now a class action plus a token migration. **Lesson: promised-but-unenforced buybacks destroy trust.** [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/tokens/launchcoin-price-drops-as-developers-switch-to-believe-token), [ClaimDepot](https://www.claimdepot.com/cases/believe-crypto-class-action-accuses-founder-ben-pasternak-of-misleading-token-investors)

### Zora / creator coins on Base
- The Base App relaunch (Jul 2025) drove 1.6M coins, about 3M traders and $470M in volume. Through 2026 it then **collapsed about 99%**: daily volume was $112k on 15 Jul 2026 versus a $63M peak. Jesse Pollak publicly called the onchain-social bet a failure. **Lesson: creator coins with no value floor go to zero, and the narrative died with them.** [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/jesse-pollak-base-zora-social-bet/), [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2025/08/03/base-takes-solana-s-crown-in-token-creation-as-coinbase-s-socialfi-ignites-zora-boom)

### Clanker (Base / Farcaster) and AI agents
- Clanker launches a token when you tag a bot on Farcaster. Fee split is 1% per swap, 40% to the creator. Weekly fees hit a record **$8M in Feb 2026**, driven by the "moltbook" AI-agent trend: 12,971 tokens in one day, deployed by autonomous agents. [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/articles/clanker-protocol-reaches-8-million-weekly-fee-milestone-as-ai-agent-social-trading-ignites-base)
- **Virtuals** rebuilt its launch model three times: Genesis points → Unicorn → modular 2026. Each rebuild targeted snipers and cabals with contribution-based allocation and auto-refunds. [PANews](https://www.panewslab.com/en/articles/177rf0tp), [Datawallet](https://www.datawallet.com/crypto/what-is-virtuals-protocol)
- **Bankr:** agents launch tokens on Base and Solana. **x402** has processed 138M agent payments on Base, 87% of them under $0.10. Spending goes to inference, data feeds and token launches. [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/10/14/ai-agents-make-138-million-crypto-payments-as-u-s-dominates-spending-on-ai-models), [TokenPost](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/technology/31826)

### four.meme (BNB)
- Launch costs about $3. Its new **Stock Memes** are paired against bStocks (for example NVDAb), and fees route into a "SkillRoyalty" vault. [Coingabbar](https://www.coingabbar.com/en/crypto-currency-news/bnb-news-today-four-meme-stock-memes-launch-update), [CryptoSlate review](https://cryptoslate.com/launchpads/four-meme-review/)

### Hyperliquid / HyperEVM
- Active launchpads: LiquidLaunch, HypurrFun, hyper.meme and others. LiquidLaunch's distinctive feature is that tokens can graduate to a **Hyperliquid spot auction** (HIP-1), not only to an AMM. [LiquidLaunch](https://x.com/LiquidLaunchHL/status/1892982849550111012), [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/liquidlaunch)

### Robinhood Chain (mainnet 1 Jul 2026)
- Built for tokenized stocks, but memecoins took over. At peak there were ~18.6k-25k launches per day, and launchpad volume reached $600M/day by 1 Sep (Pons ~60%, LONG ~25%). Tokenized equities fell from 33% to 6% of TVL. [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/07/13/robinhood-built-a-blockchain-for-tokenized-stocks-memecoins-took-over), [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/robinhood-chain-memecoins-tokenized-stocks/)
- **Noxa** (largest early launchpad) made about $12M in fees ($3M/day at peak), then shut down on 11-13 Jul. CASHCAT fell 30% that day. **The platform's own fragility hurt its tokens.** [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/07/15/the-launchpad-that-fueled-robinhood-chain-s-memecoin-boom-just-gave-away-all-its-revenue)
- **Stock-paired memecoins** (pools paired against TSLA, NVDA or HIMS stock tokens) peaked at about $440M/day, and are reported to move the underlying stock tokens. [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/robinhood-stock-paired-meme-coins), [Cryptobriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/memecoins-tokenized-stocks-price-impact/)
- **Tesoro/TORO** puts a 3% trade tax into tokenized gold and equities held in a shared treasury. This is the closest public analogue to ARC PULSE's "tax → backing", but the backing is not redeemable. [CryptoTicker](https://cryptoticker.io/en/robinhood-chain-memecoins-explained/)
- **Pools.trade** (Uniswap Labs) has zero fees and permanently locked v4 liquidity, yet earns only $45k/week vs Pons at $3.1M. **Safety alone does not win distribution.** Chain fees fell 62% after gas subsidies ended on 29 Sep. [CryptoTicker](https://cryptoticker.io/en/robinhood-chain-memecoins-explained/)

### Arc (mainnet 16 Sep 2026)
- Launchpads took **82% of $410.8M in day-one DEX volume**. Arguspad did $202M and minted 83,751 tokens (86% of all tokens created). Minara.fun did $36M and Tollylabs $19.7M. Day one was 28x Robinhood Chain's first day. [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/crypto/articles/meme-coin-launchpads-captured-82-072853066.html)

The creator routes the remaining 90% to USDC for themselves, buyback/burn, USDC dividends to holders, or liquidity. Smithii already sells an "Argus bundler with dev buy and snipers". [GitHub argus-world](https://github.com/arguspad/argus-world), [Smithii](https://smithii.io/en/argus-bundler-arc/)
  - **Implication:** "Locked full-supply pool + USDC fees" is no longer unique on Arc. ARC PULSE's real moat is the **redeemable floor vault**, which Argus does not have, plus multi-chain reach.
- Other Arc launchpads: Bullcheese (Forbes), ubi.fun, ARCLaunch (pre-launch). [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/boazsobrado/2026/09/16/circle-built-arc-for-blackrock-memecoins-are-moving-in-first/), [ubi.fun](https://ubi.fun/)
- Arc natively supports **USYC**, a tokenized T-bill money-market fund, as a first-class yield asset. [Arc docs](https://docs.arc.io/arc/concepts/stablecoin-native-model)

### Monad, Move chains

- Sui and Aptos have MovePump, Moonbags (fees shared with creators, holders and stakers) and SuiPump. These are smaller markets and less contested. [Suipiens](https://suipiens.com/blog/exploring-meme-launchpads-on-sui-blockchain/)

## 2. What users complain about most, and what is still unsolved

| Problem | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Snipers/bundlers at launch** | One ring took $18.43M from 53 Robinhood Chain launches (Jul-Sep 2026) by sniping 70%+ of supply with 70-200 wallets. On **Pons V2**, the creator can exempt up to 32 addresses from the 99% anti-snipe tax, and the ring used exactly that waiver. [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/defi/2026-09-27-onchain-analyst-links-18-4-million-in-robinhood-chain-memecoin-extractions-to-single-rug-pull-operation-416960), [CryptoTimes](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/09/28/analyst-exposes-18-43m-robinhood-chain-memecoin-rug-pull-ring/) | **Unsolved.** Launchpads still sell bundling as a feature, and Smithii sells bundlers for Argus too. |
| **Rugs / dead tokens** | 98.6% of pump.fun's 11.9M tokens were rugged or abandoned. Chainalysis: $2.8B in rugs in 2025. RICO class action cleared to proceed (31 Aug 2026), alleging $4-5.5B in losses. [Coinlaw](https://coinlaw.io/memecoin-statistics/), [Wolf Popper](https://www.wolfpopper.com/news/court-allows-rico-claims-against-pumpfun-and-founders-to-proceed), [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/331528/solana-pump-fun-amended-rico-suit-alleging-meme-coin-gambling) | LP-pull rugs are mostly solved through locked LP. **"Soft rugs" (dev or insider dumps, going to zero) are unsolved.** |
| **Fake volume / wash trading** | Bots are 60-80% of pump.fun volume. 21.4% of trades in one dataset were wash trades. DOJ charged 10 people at market makers (Mar 2026). [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/trading-bots-pump-fun-solana-meme-coins/), [arXiv MemeTrans](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.13480v1) | **Unsolved.** No launchpad shows "real" volume. |
| **Post-graduation liquidity cliff** | Virtual reserves vanish at migration and curve buyers dump. Graduation rate is 0.2-0.26%. [Blofin](https://blofin.com/en/academy/education/solana/solana-memecoins-and-pump-fun-explained), [repyl](https://repyl.substack.com/p/memecoin-trading-strategy-market) | Patched (BOOST) but not solved. **ARC PULSE has no migration step, so it avoids this by design.** |
| **Clones / copycat tickers** | Clones of CASHCAT, Hood and Scatman on Robinhood Chain. 29 pools touching CYBERLEEK, with a fake that fell 94% in a day. [U.Today](https://u.today/beware-of-memecoin-scams-on-robinhood-chain-scatman-hood-and-cashcat-copycats-heres-how-to-avoid), [CryptoTicker](https://cryptoticker.io/en/robinhood-chain-memecoins-explained/) | **Unsolved, and worse on new chains.** |
| **Holder concentration / insider cabals** | "40% on ten addresses" is common. [CryptoTicker](https://cryptoticker.io/en/robinhood-chain-memecoins-explained/) | Partly addressed (Virtuals points). |
| **Platform risk** | Noxa disappeared, Believe never delivered its promised buybacks. | **Unsolved:** promises are off-chain. |
| **"Most users lose"** | Admitted in pump.fun's internal messages. [DL News](https://www.dlnews.com/articles/defi/pump-fun-exec-says-most-lose-on-platform-per-lawsuit/) | Structural. Cashback and holder rewards are cosmetic fixes. |
| **Creator coin collapse** | Zora -99%. | **Unsolved:** nothing gives creator coins a floor. |
| **Cross-chain reputation hopping** | Bad actors burn one chain's reputation and move to another. [bex.co](https://bex.co/blog/2026/04/20/meme-launchpad-2-pump-fun-letsbonk-anti-sniper-reputation) | Only "emerging". |

**Main takeaway:** Every competitor sells something that **promises** safety: shields, points, buybacks. Nobody **proves** a minimum value onchain. Baseline Markets has a rising, redeemable floor ("BLV") but is a single-token DeFi protocol (~$1.6M TVL), not a memecoin factory ([Baseline](https://www.baseline.markets/faq), [DefiLlama](https://defillama.com/protocol/baseline-protocol)). Tesoro has backing but it is not redeemable. **A redeemable, only-rising USDC floor on every token, across 11 chains, is unclaimed territory.**

## 3. New ideas that fit ARC PULSE's mechanics

Each idea lists: why it would get attention / how hard it is on our stack / risks.

**A. Floor Proof everywhere ("Never Zero" badge, Floor Card, Near-Floor screener)**
- *Why:* This turns the vault into a viral, screenshot-able number. Every token page, tweet card and embed shows "Floor $0.00041, up 37% since launch, never fell". A "Near-Floor" screener lists tokens trading within X% of their redeemable floor, which can only lose a small amount and can only rise, so it reads as a "free lottery ticket" for degens. A Floor-ATH leaderboard adds to that. It speaks directly to the "98.6% rug" and Zora -99% narrative.
- *Effort:* Low. The data is already onchain, so this is frontend, OG images, an indexer and a GENESIS feed.
- *Risks:* A low floor next to a high price can look like a guarantee. Use wording such as "redeemable floor", not "safe". This is a regulatory perception risk, because "redeemable for USDC" can look like a fund product.

**B. Zero-exemption launch + public "Bundle Map" at block 0**
- *Why:* The $18.4M Robinhood Chain ring used Pons V2's 32-address tax exemption. ARC PULSE's founder pays the same tax as everyone else. Turn that into a marketing weapon: an immutable no-whitelist rule, plus GENESIS publishing a funding-cluster graph of the first N blocks' buyers on every token page (a "Bundle score").
- *Effort:* Low to medium. The contract rule already exists. The cluster analysis needs a funding-source indexer per chain.
- *Risks:* False positives in clustering could cause defamation-style complaints. Bundlers will adapt with fresh wallets funded through CEXs.

**C. Canonical-address anti-clone badge (same address on 9 chains)**
- *Why:* Clone tickers are the #1 new-chain scam. If the real token has the same contract address on Arc, Base, Robinhood Chain and others, that address becomes a cross-chain identity. Add a "Verified canonical: 0xABC… on 9 chains" badge plus a public resolver API that wallets and DexScreener can query. oftpad.fun tried omnichain launches and has zero tokens, so nobody actually owns this.
- *Effort:* Low to medium. LayerZero spread already ships, so this is a registry contract or view plus an API.
- *Risks:* LayerZero DVN/config security (a misconfigured peer can mint). Supply accounting across chains has to be shown clearly. **Floor per chain vs global floor must be explained**: whether the vault is per chain or bridged is a design decision that users will get confused about.

**D. RWA/stock-collateral memes on Robinhood Chain and BNB ("paste TSLA as collateral")**
- *Why:* Stock-paired memes did $440M/day, Tesoro taxes into gold, and four.meme has Stock Memes. Nobody gives a meme a **redeemable** floor in a stock or gold token. With paste-any-CA collateral, the pitch is "$CYBERTRUCK, floor backed by TSLA tokens". It rides the hottest Robinhood Chain narrative.
- *Effort:* Low, since the paste-CA collateral already exists. Main work is curated presets and price display.
- *Risks:* Stock tokens may have transfer, KYC or geo restrictions; check whether vault contracts can hold them. The floor becomes volatile because it is denominated in the stock. Legal optics around securities.

**E. Yield-bearing floor (USYC on Arc, Aave/Morpho elsewhere)**
- *Why:* "The floor rises even when nobody trades." Vault USDC earns T-bill yield (USYC is native on Arc), and the yield accrues to the floor. This is the first memecoin that earns interest. It is also a strong Circle and Arc ecosystem story, with possible co-marketing or grants.
- *Effort:* Medium. You need a vault strategy adapter, redemption buffer logic and accounting. USYC access may require allowlisting, so check Hashnote/Circle terms.
- *Risks:* Yield-source risk (depeg, protocol hack, USYC permissioning). Redemption liquidity when funds are deployed. Bigger regulatory risk, because yield plus redeemability looks even more like a security or fund. Mitigate with a liquid buffer and opt-in per token.

**F. No-liquidation loans against the floor**
- *Why:* "Borrow USDC against your memecoin, never liquidated." The loan is capped at floor value, and the token is locked as collateral. If you don't repay, the protocol simply redeems it at the floor. Users get liquidity without selling, and the price is not dumped. Baseline offers floor-borrowing for one token; nobody does it for memecoins at scale.
- *Effort:* Medium. A small lending module per vault. Risk is bounded because loan ≤ floor.
- *Risks:* Interest-rate and fee design. Interaction with floor accounting (borrowed USDC leaves the vault). Audit needed.

**G. Deployer Passport (cross-chain, soulbound reputation computed by GENESIS)**
- *Why:* This addresses "dev dumps" and chain-hopping scammers. A per-wallet record covers: tokens launched across all 11 chains, floor growth, the share of their own supply sold in the first 24h, holder retention, and bundle scores. Show it on every launch, and sell it to other launchpads and wallets as a GENESIS API. LetsBonk has internal points; nobody has a portable, cross-chain one.
- *Effort:* Medium. Needs an indexer across chains, a scoring model and an attestation (EAS or SBT).
- *Risks:* Sybil (new wallet = clean slate, so scores should be "unknown", not "good"). Gaming. Fairness disputes.

**H. Agent launch API + GENESIS paid via x402**
- *Why:* Agent launches produced Clanker's $8M/week record, and x402 has 138M payments with "token launches" as a named spend category. Expose (1) an x402-paid endpoint where an agent launches a floor-backed token in one call, and (2) GENESIS metrics (floor, bundle score, real volume) sold per call in USDC on Arc, its native habitat. The pitch: "agents launch tokens that can't go to zero."
- *Effort:* Medium. HTTP/x402 server, an MCP tool, and rate and abuse limits.
- *Risks:* Spam launches (needs a minimum launch fee). Agent-launched tokens attract bad press if they are scams. Data-quality liability.

**I. Floor-backed creator / stream coins**
- *Why:* Livestreams are the biggest 2026 hype driver (pump.fun), and creator coins died because they had no floor (Zora). Combine them: part of the creator fee goes to the floor while the creator streams. Use embeds (Twitch, X, YouTube, Kick), not your own video infrastructure. Show "Floor rises while live", with tip-to-floor buttons.
- *Effort:* Medium to high, mostly product and BD for creators. Use embeds plus social login to keep it manageable.
- *Risks:* Content moderation (pump.fun streams had extreme content incidents), creator onboarding cost, and the creator fee competing with floor share.

**J. USDC-native one-tap onboarding: Telegram mini-app + social/passkey login, gas in USDC**
- *Why:* Mobile is 36% of Solana volume and Telegram bots dominate the flow. On Arc, gas *is* USDC, so "sign in with Google, deposit USDC, buy" works with no gas token. That onboarding edge is unique to Arc.
- *Effort:* Medium. Embedded wallet (Privy, Dynamic or Circle Wallets) plus a Telegram Mini App.
- *Risks:* Custody and key-management liability, plus the compliance of fiat on-ramps.

**Further ideas (not in the top 10):**
- **K.** "Floor Wars" attention markets: bet on which token's floor grows most this week (Kaito/Polymarket-style). Medium effort, gambling and regulatory exposure.
- **L.** "Real volume" meter: GENESIS strips out wash and self-trades and shows organic volume. Medium effort, a strong differentiator against the 60-80% bot volume.
- **M.** Cross-chain FTHM burn tracker / "burn clock" for PulseSwap. Low effort, low but steady hype.
- **N.** "No migration cliff" messaging: there is no graduation, so there is no graduation dump. Marketing only.

**Strategic notes:**
- **Arguspad already copies the locked full-supply pool and USDC-fee model on Arc.** Lead every message with the **redeemable, only-rising floor**, which no competitor has.
- **Publish safety rules as immutable contract facts, not promises.** Believe and Noxa show that off-chain promises and platform fragility destroy trust.
- **Pools.trade shows that safety alone does not win** ($45k/week vs Pons at $3.1M). Pair safety with a game loop: the Near-Floor screener, Floor-ATH events and creator streams.
