# ARC PULSE: Ecosystem Opportunities (researched 16 Oct 2026)

Scope: grants, builder programs, listings, ecosystem gaps and low-cost visibility for ARC PULSE (multi-chain memecoin launchpad; X @Arcdepthoock, 7 followers; arc-depth.github.io).
Method: web search and page fetches, read-only. Nothing was applied for or posted. Items marked **(unverified)** came from secondary sources or could not be confirmed on the official page.

---

## 0. Key findings first

1. **Closed or not a fit:** most of the big October programs have already closed. These are Arbitrum Open House Singapore (buildathon closed 4 Oct), Monad Metropolis (closed 13 Oct), Colosseum Crypto World's Fair (closed 12 Oct), Arc Microgrants (closed 14/15 Oct), Base Batches 004 (closed 9 Sep) and HYPE Singapore (6-7 Oct). Do not chase them. Watch for their next rounds instead.
2. **Arc is not empty, it is crowded.** About 18 launchpads applied to become "Arc's pump.fun". On day one, launchpads made up 82% of Arc's $410M DEX volume, and Arguspad alone did $202M and created 86% of all new tokens. Pump.fun is also live on or exploring Arc. Other Arc launchpads include eve.fun, Flipt, lolpad, NebulaPad, onmi.fun, sashimi.fun, UBI.FUN, Bullcheese, ARCLaunch and SolonPad. The "first memecoin launchpad on Arc" pitch is gone.
3. **Robinhood Chain is also crowded.** Pons handles about $3.1M of weekly fees. Other launchpads there are hood.fun, Pools.trade (from Uniswap Labs), heks, Flap and Robinlaunch. Weekly launchpad fees fell about 80% after gas rebates ended on 29 Sep. This is a weak moment to enter as "another launchpad", but the dominant player is losing momentum.
4. **Monad, HyperEVM, Sui and Aptos each already have an incumbent:** nad.fun ($1.1M seed), Hypurr.fun / LiquidLaunch / hyper.meme, Move Pump and emojicoin.fun. The real gap is not a missing chain. It is **one UI that launches the same meme across 11 chains with USDC-quoted pairs and stablecoin gas on Arc.** No competitor found offers that.
5. **Name collision:** "Arc Pulse" is the trademarked product line of Arc, a Dutch phone-case company (arc.cc). It sells cases, runs a creator program, and dominates search results for "Arc Pulse". Your X handle (@Arcdepthoock) and domain (arc-depth) also do not match the brand. This hurts discoverability, and it could become a legal problem. Decide on one consistent brand and handle before spending effort on directory listings.
6. **The cheapest, highest-leverage free traffic is a DefiLlama dimension-adapter PR.** Arc and Robinhood launchpads (ArgusPad, heks) got theirs merged within days in Sep 2026, and `CHAIN.ARC` is supported. After that, use the free directories (ArcLens, Arc Index, Sui Directory, MonadVision, base.dev).

## 3. Low-cost visibility tactics (2026, no KOL spend)

1. **Build in public with numbers.** Post daily metrics (launches, graduations, fees per chain), a weekly thread, and a short video clip of a launch in under 30 seconds. Data posts are what Arc and Robinhood ecosystem accounts quote. Aim for 8-15 posts per week.
2. **Reply-guy strategy on ecosystem accounts.** Within minutes, give substantive replies (with screenshots of a token launched on your platform) under @arc, @circle, @RobinhoodApp/Robinhood Chain posts, @base, @monad and @SuiNetwork. With only 7 followers, borrowed reach is the main lever.
3. **Join ecosystem X Communities** for Arc, Robinhood Chain, Base, Monad and Sui, and the Farcaster channels /base, /memes, /monad and /sui (check which are active). Post one useful item per week, not shills.
4. **Arc House forum + Architects.** Write one guest article ("How a USDC-gas launchpad works: curve math on Arc"). It is worth 500 points and gets you seen by Circle DevRel, the same people who review grants.
5. **Creator-first loop.** Give every token a ready-made share card and an X link that tags @Arcdepthoock. Pay a referral share of fees (most competitors already do). Each launch becomes free distribution.
6. **Open-source contributions as marketing:** DefiLlama adapter, Panora token-list PR, a Dune dashboard, and an open-sourced audited curve contract. Each one is a public artifact you can announce.
7. **Hackathon presence over hackathon wins.** Submit even small extensions (e.g. "cross-chain launch via CCTP") to the next Arc / Open House / Monad Blitz events. Judges and sponsor DevRel retweet participants.
8. **X Spaces as a guest, not a host.** Ask small ecosystem hosts (Arc community Spaces, Robinhood Chain degen Spaces) for a 5-minute slot. Hosting with 7 followers does not work.
9. **Skip for now:** paid DexScreener boosts, Enhanced Token Info ($299/token), GeckoTerminal Express ($10k) and paid KOLs.

## 4. Top 10 prioritized actions

1. **Fix the brand collision first (½ day).** "Arc Pulse" is a phone-case trademark (arc.cc), and @Arcdepthoock / arc-depth do not match the name. Choose one name, rename the X handle (keeping the account, followers and history), and add a matching domain or GitHub Pages path. Every listing below depends on this.
2. **Ship a DefiLlama fees adapter (2-4 days).** Fork DefiLlama/dimension-adapters and copy the structure of `fees/arguspad/index.ts` (Arc) and the heks adapter (Robinhood). Include all fee sources (curve trades, launch fee, post-graduation LP fee), set the double-counting flag if graduated pools sit on Uniswap, and run `npm test fees <slug>`. Then open the PR and add a `dexs` adapter for curve volume.
3. **Submit to GeckoTerminal and the DexScreener Discord (1 day).** Use the free form at about.geckoterminal.com/dex-chain-listing for the launchpad on each EVM chain (state the bonding-curve contract and graduation target), and make a DEX/launchpad listing request in the DexScreener Discord. Pay for nothing.
4. **Register on base.dev and integrate a Builder Code (½ day).** Fill in the metadata (icon, screenshots, category) and append the Builder Code to Base transactions. This makes you eligible for the Base App store, leaderboards and future rewards. Keep shipping in public for a Base Builder Grant nomination.
5. **Register on Avalanche Retro9000 (1 hour).** Register the project at retro9000.avax.network, then ask users to connect their wallet and verify X so they count as Verified-tier users. New projects get a 5x multiplier.
6. **File the free directory submissions (1 day total):** ArcLens, Arc Index, arc.io/ecosystem form, MonadVision, Sui Directory, and the Panora token-list PR plus attestation tweet for Aptos tokens and FTHM-on-Aptos if it exists.
7. **Prepare a grant packet now and apply to Aptos Ecosystem Grants (3 days).** Write a 1-page deck, metrics, milestones and repo link. Aptos is your least-contested chain, grants are $5-50k, and you can ask grants@aptosfoundation.org about meme/consumer fit first. Reuse the same packet, pitched as USDC-utility via CCTP cross-chain launches, for Circle Developer Grants (circle.questbook.app).
8. **Watch the Robinhood Chain builder funding announcement (Tenev, 13 Oct) and Arbitrum Open House's next city.** Set alerts on robinhood.com/newsroom, openhouse.arbitrum.io and @arbitrum. Have the packet from step 7 ready so you can apply on day one. One top-3 spot is reserved for Robinhood Chain projects.
9. **Get FTHM onto CoinGecko, then CMC (1 day plus about 5 days review).** Make sure FTHM has a pool on a CoinGecko-tracked DEX with real liquidity, verify the contract on the explorers, publish a circulating-supply endpoint, and submit through the CoinGecko request form (free; never use paid "listing agents").
10. **Run a 30-day build-in-public plus Dune routine.** Publish a dashboard (launches, graduations, fees per chain), post daily stats and a weekly thread, reply under ecosystem accounts, write one Arc House guest article, and join the X Communities and Farcaster channels. Stay ready for the next Arc Microgrants round, Colosseum Eternal sprint and Monad Momentum wave. All three favour live products with public repos.
